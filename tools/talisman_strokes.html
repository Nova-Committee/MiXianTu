Talisman strokes

Author the pattern.strokes of a mxt:talisman_drawing recipe: one polyline per stroke, every point normalised to [0,1]² against the fixed 90 × 210 canvas. This tool covers the shape only — the recipe still needs its talisman, result and whatever else it declares. Scoring normalises translation and scale away but not rotation, so where the shape sits and how big it is do not matter, while a turned one does.

    Paste this as the value of pattern.strokes. The scorer resamples every — and drops detail finer than 0.5 × tolerance — — at the default tolerance —, so points closer together than that are decoration. The brush is charged about one pigment point per pixel of drawn length.
