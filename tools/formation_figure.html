Formation figure

Author the figure an array draws out of the only two primitives the renderer can sample: polylines and circles. Coordinates are in units of the array's own radius, so 1 is exactly the ring the array's radius draws, and one figure scales with every array that reads it. Growth is the game's rule and not a tool setting — every stroke is cut at progress × its own length, so the ring and the star grow together — so the slider and Play show what the client would be drawing. What it writes is one mxt:formation_shape entry: save it as data/<namespace>/mxt/formation_shape/<name>.json and name it from an mxt:particle module's shape. The ring plus a five-pointed star is mxt:five, one of the three the mod ships itself.

    A figure is a strokes list of {"type": "mxt:polyline", "points": [[x, z], …]} and {"type": "mxt:circle", "radius": 1}, whose optional center defaults to the controller and may sit anywhere. A circle starts due east unless start_angle (degrees, positive towards +Z) says otherwise; a polyline starts at its first point. A stroke asks for ceil(length in blocks / its spacing) + 1 points, so this figure wants — at —, and one stroke is capped at — points, after which its far end is dropped. Every stroke carries its own paint — "color": "#80c0ff", or "gradient": [{"at": 0, "color": "#80c0ff"}, {"at": 1, "color": "#ffffff"}] run along its own length from its first point to its last — and there is no figure-wide colour to fall back to, so one of the two is required. The strip above is that ramp: click it to add a stop, drag a marker to move one. start and end are the window of the draw-in this stroke belongs to: the figure's progress is mapped onto it, so start: 0.5 keeps a stroke for the second half. The last two belong to the stroke as well: spacing is how many blocks apart its dusts sit and size how big each one is drawn, both standard (0.33 and 2) unless it says otherwise. Play runs the draw-in the way the game plays it — duration_ticks ticks at 20 a second — and the speed slider and the loop belong to the preview only. Of everything on this page only radius, the speed and the loop never reach the file, and that is because radius is the array's: it is here so the count and the dust size are honest.
